# EDA notebook outline

Use this outline in a Jupyter notebook or Google Colab:

1. Import pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.
2. Load `data/customer_feedback.csv` or upload your dataset.
3. Inspect shape, columns, data types, duplicates, and missing values.
4. Normalize column names and standardize channel labels.
5. Clean empty feedback and duplicate records.
6. Parse dates and convert ratings to numeric values.
7. Analyze feedback counts over time and by channel.
8. Compare average ratings by channel.
9. Calculate sentiment scores and compare sentiment by channel.
10. Examine feedback length and frequent words.
11. Summarize patterns, limitations, and recommendations.
12. Export the cleaned dataset and visualizations.
