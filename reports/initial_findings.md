# Initial findings template

Replace these statements with observations after running the analysis on your actual dataset.

- **Channel coverage:** Which channels contribute the most feedback?
- **Satisfaction:** Which channels have the highest and lowest average rating?
- **Sentiment:** Is negative feedback concentrated in any channel or time period?
- **Recurring themes:** What issues recur most often (e.g., payments, login, delivery, support)?
- **Data quality:** What percentage of records were missing text, dates, or ratings?
- **Actions:** Which issue should be prioritized, and what evidence supports the recommendation?

Do not present the synthetic sample as evidence about real customers.
